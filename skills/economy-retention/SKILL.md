# Skill: Economy, Retention & Monetization

Use for currencies, rewards, permanent progression, daily systems, ads, IAP concepts and progression pacing.

## Commercial principle
Retention comes from meaningful unfinished goals, not artificial friction. Monetization should amplify value at natural decision points.

## Economy structure
Keep the launch economy small:
- run currency: temporary, spent during the run;
- meta currency: persistent, earned through play and milestones;
- optional premium/payment layer only when justified by a portal.

Avoid five currencies, opaque conversion chains and pay-to-fix intentionally bad balance.

## Rewarded ads
Good placements: revive, double post-boss reward, reroll, bonus chest, optional extra daily attempt. The player must know the reward before watching.

## Interstitials
Only at natural breaks such as run end, major boss/result transitions or other portal-compliant pauses. Never interrupt active arrangement or combat.

## Retention systems
Prioritize cheap systemic retention:
- recipe/item discovery book;
- daily seeded challenge;
- endless high score;
- achievements/milestones;
- rotating modifiers.

## Progression rule
Permanent progression should primarily unlock possibilities and convenience, not stack endless +1% stat bonuses that trivialize mastery.

## Metrics to instrument later
Tutorial completion, first-boss reach, run duration, boss loss reasons, item pick rates, synergy completion, rewarded-ad opt-in, D1/D7 return, session count and ARPDAU where the platform exposes it.
